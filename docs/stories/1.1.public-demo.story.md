## Story

**As a** product-oriented visitor, **I want** to explore and scoop a synthetic fashion catalog, **so that** I can assess the product concept without orders, tracking, or email collection.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The public brand is Scoopy and the demo supports filters, themes, scoop size, and a visible selection result.
2. The catalog is synthetic and contains no third-party images, hotlinks, source URLs, or private business data.
3. The waitlist is explicitly demo-only and performs no storage or network request.
4. README, case study, limitations, Pages workflow, and separated MIT/code versus brand/asset rights are present.
5. Desktop/mobile, keyboard, reduced-motion, contrast, no-network, and secret checks pass.
