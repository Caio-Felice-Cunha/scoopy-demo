## Story

**As a** recruiter or product engineer, **I want** to experience the complete Scoopy website and inspect how it was built, **so that** I can evaluate both product craft and engineering depth.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The complete tracked `clara/clarify/website` experience is adapted into Scoopy without modifying the source prototype.
2. The site preserves its 3D scroll narrative, collections, scoop tiers, builder, responsive behavior, reduced-motion fallback, and no-WebGL fallback.
3. The public catalog contains exactly 221 deterministic synthetic items across 12 themes and no third-party product assets, URLs, or commercial claims.
4. The waitlist is visibly demonstrative and performs no storage or network request.
5. Runtime libraries and fonts are pinned and served locally; the demo makes no third-party runtime request.
6. `/case-study/` explains tokens, architecture, catalog logic, progressive enhancement, security, testing, limitations, and local execution using real public snippets.
7. Unit, desktop/mobile Playwright, keyboard, reduced-motion, no-network, contrast, secret, and PII gates pass.
