Our story

Cute, but make it thoughtful.

There's a lot of cute stationery out there — piled on giant shelves, hit or miss, a bit overwhelming. Scoopy started with a simple idea: what if someone with really good taste just picked the best bits for you?

The public demo tests that question with generated inventory, deterministic selection, and a tactile reveal — without copying a retailer catalog or pretending to accept an order.

Designed as a product and interaction study in Vancouver. 🤍
